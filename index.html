Questions

Straight answers.

Do you give signals or picks? +

No. Never. Faceless Wicks is education: how to read a chart, not what to buy or when. There are no signals, alerts, or "setups of the day," and there never will be.

Is this financial advice? +

No. Everything here is for education and general information only. It isn't financial advice, and nothing on a chart is guaranteed. Make your own decisions, and consider a licensed professional for your own situation.

Do I need any experience? +

None. The whole point is to explain the basics most channels skip, plainly, one idea at a time, so a candle actually makes sense when you look at one. Start with the three lessons above.

What does it cost? +

Nothing. The channels are free to follow and the email list is free to join. If that ever changes, you'll hear it from us first.
